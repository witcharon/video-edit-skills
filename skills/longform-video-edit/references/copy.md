# On-screen copy

When the copywriting skill is installed, load it for every on-screen line. Its "No AI Tells" rules apply. Beyond those:

- **Only spoken words, or the product's real UI copy.**
  - A headline is built from the sentence the speaker said, in their order.
  - A UI is rebuilt with the text the product really shows.
- **No invented proof.** Never show a number, claim, logo, badge or verified tick the video does not say or the account does not have.
- **Short.**
  - Headline: a small line of up to ~4 words plus one big word or number.
  - Caption: a small line plus 1–2 key words.
  - Tail: up to ~3 words.
  - If it doesn't fit beside the face at the planned size, cut words, not size.
- **Drop contrast reveals** ("not X, but Y" / "X değil, Y") when the key part stands alone. Show Y with its own words: the speech already carries the contrast.
- **Mirror hedges.** If they say "let's say 100 customers", show "let's say 100 customers" (or "diyelim ki 100 müşteri"), not "100 customers".
- **Language and case.**
  - Use the video's language.
  - Write the final case into the text itself. CSS `text-transform: uppercase` turns Turkish i into I, not İ.
- **Spelling of names.** Use the corrected spelling of products and people (the transcript's ASR may be wrong). Keep a list of fixes in `project.md`.
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks** unless the brand uses them.
